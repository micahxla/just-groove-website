Privacy Policy

DJ Groove respects your privacy. Information submitted through this website is used only to respond to event inquiries, provide quotes, communicate about services, and manage potential or confirmed bookings.

If you would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you previously submitted, contact DJ Groove at justgroovellc@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time as the website and services change.
