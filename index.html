01 — About

MORE THAN MUSIC. IT'S AN EXPERIENCE.

Christopher Harrell, known professionally as DJ Groove, is an accomplished DJ and emcee with more than two decades of experience and over 1,000 events to his credit. His approach combines thoughtful music curation with an instinct for reading the room, creating a sound that feels personal to each occasion.

As a former professional choreographer, Groove brings a dancer’s understanding of rhythm, timing, and movement to the DJ booth. His love of dance shapes how he selects music, builds energy, and responds to a crowd. He understands how people move to music and how the right selection can draw them together on the dance floor. Blending timeless favorites with music across genres and generations, Groove tailors each set to the guests, the atmosphere, and the host’s vision. As an emcee, he brings a confident, polished presence that keeps the celebration flowing naturally. His experience spans resort residencies, corporate entertainment, and major sporting events, grounded in a personal connection to both music and dance.
